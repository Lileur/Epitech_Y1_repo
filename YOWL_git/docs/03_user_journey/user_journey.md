## Context

Steve uses social media daily to relax, discover content, and interact with others.
He wants a smooth and enjoyable experience, without interruptions or irrelevant content.


| Step | User action              | Thoughts & feelings                  | Pain point                         | Opportunity                      |
| ---- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
|   1  | Opens a social media app | I want to relax and scroll for a bit | Expects interruptions              | Offer a calm, ad-free experience |
|   2  | Starts scrolling the feed| This content looks interesting       | Feed mixes relevant posts with ads | Show only content, no ads        |
|   3  | An ad appears            | Again? This breaks the flow          | Ads interrupt the experience       | Remove ads completely/option to skip immediately |
|   4  | Watches or skips the ad  | This is wasting my time              | Ads are long or unskippable        | Respect user’s time              |
|   5  | Continues scrolling      | I’m less engaged now                 | Loss of interest after interruption | Keep continuous scrolling       |
|   6  | Gets irrelevant recommendations | This is not what I’m looking for | Poor content relevance          | Improve feed relevance           |
|   7  | Checks how long he has been scrolling | I didn’t realize how much time passed | Low sense of time    | Add better time control          |
|   8  | Leaves the app           | I’ll stop for now                    | Frustration leads to early exit    | Increase retention with better UX |


## Conclusion

Steve’s journey shows how too much advertising and interruptions negatively affect the user experience. 
By removing ads and improving feed relevance, the platform can keep users engaged longer and create a more enjoyable experience.
