During interviews, the problem that got brought up the most was certainly the amount of ads in social apps they got daily as well as their length and the fact that some of them were unskippable. One of the interviewed reported finding a workaround for this problem using an adblock but admitted that it didn't solve the original problem since most social apps actually found out how to stop adblocks. Other than this person the others said that this ads were annoying, infuriating even, with apparently no sign that this problem will ever be fixed.
