Objective:

We want to prevent excess advertising on social medias.

Assumptions:

1. Get a paid subscription to remove ads.

2. Putting a skip button directly at the start of the ad.

3. Making a paid social media application so we don't have to put any ads in it.

Risks:

1. If we remove ads, the profit will be significantly lower.

Constraints:

The difficulty will be to make a user-friendly application without losing money.
