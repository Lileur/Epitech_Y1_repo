# Marketing Strategy and KPIs

## Our Target Audience
Our chatbot is designed for businesses. We are targeting two types of people:
* **Professionals:** Those whose job is to organize events or seminars for other companies
* **Individuals:** Those who sometimes need to organize an end-of-year dinner or an event, but are not used to doing it

## The Needs
Organizing an event is often complicated. Here are the problems our chatbot will solve:
* **A huge waste of time:** Finding a suitable venue/location requires research that can be time-consuming, especially when you do not have experience in this field.
* **A real headache:** It can be overwhelming to quickly find a place that checks all the boxes (the right city, large enough for the number of people attending the event, matching the venue’s availability with the desired dates)

## Objective of our Chatbot
Our chatbot is a virtual assistant that searches for venues on behalf of the user. Its main goals are:
* **Making human work easier (Automation):** The bot looks through our list of venues and instantly finds those that match
* **Asking the right questions:** Through conversation, it immediately asks for the important information (How many people? Which city?)
* **Helping users choose very quickly:** To avoid overwhelming the user with a list of 50 venues, the bot will only suggest the **top 3**. If someone says *"I want a venue in Paris for 200 people with a budget of 500 euros"*, it provides the Top 3. This helps the client make a decision right away

## What it brings
* **For organizers:** Huge time savings. Instead of searching for hours, they find a venue in just a few minutes
* **For venue owners:** The chatbot generates more qualified requests. When a client gets in touch, they already know the venue matches the expected capacity, the desired date, and the type of event required

## Ethics and Responsible Use of AI

In this section, we show that we have considered the possible risks of our chatbot and how to limit them.  
The objective is to offer a useful, reliable, and user-respectful solution.

### Data Privacy
The chatbot only asks for the information necessary to search for a venue:
- city
- date
- number of people
- type of event

No unnecessary personal data is requested.
The information is only used to improve the search and suggest the most suitable venues.

### Transparency
The user clearly knows that they are interacting with a chatbot and not a human.
They understand that the recommendations are generated from the criteria they provide, such as the city, date, or number of participants.

This transparency helps create a relationship of trust with the user.

### Avoiding Bias
The recommendations are made only according to the needs expressed by the user.

The chatbot must not always highlight the same venues or favor certain places without reason.
To avoid this, ELIZA ranks results according to objective criteria:
- city
- date
- capacity
- type of event
- relevance to the request

### Decision Support
The chatbot suggests the **3 best venues** to help the user choose quickly.

However, the final choice always remains human.  
ELIZA is therefore a decision-support tool that simplifies the search without replacing the user’s judgment.
